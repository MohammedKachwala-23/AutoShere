# 🚘 Autosphere — Intelligent Car Recommendation Platform

**Autosphere** is a budget-aware, Flask-based car recommendation system built to eliminate the pressure and bias of traditional car sales. By putting control back into the hands of users, Autosphere uses financial input and smart logic to suggest cars that match a user’s needs — without pushing them beyond their budget.

---

## 💡 Why Autosphere?

Buying a car can be overwhelming, especially when salespeople influence buyers into stretching their budget. **Autosphere** addresses this by offering:
- Transparent, unbiased car recommendations.
- Personalized results based on real financial data.
- Full control over car exploration, comparison, and decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🔍 Personalized Car Recommendations
- After login, users input their monthly financial details.
- The system analyzes affordability using smart calculations.
- **15** recommended cars are displayed based on user's budget and preferences.

### 🧠 Smart Car Search
- Search by **Make**, **Model**, or  **Year**.
- Responsive and fast search results for specific vehicle needs.

### 👤 User Profile Management
- Users can update:
  - Username
  - Email address
  - Password

### 📞 Contact & About Pages
- **Contact Us** page dedicated to contact details.
- **Why Autosphere?** page explaining the vision behind the project.

### 🛠 Admin Panel
- Admins can:
  - Add new cars to the inventory.
  - View all listed cars.
  - Delete vehicles as needed.

---

## 🧑‍💻 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS, Jinja Templates  
- **Backend:** Python, Flask  
- **Database:**  Neo4j  
- **Authentication:** Flask-WTF, Session Management  
- **Tools:** Custom Financial Calculator
